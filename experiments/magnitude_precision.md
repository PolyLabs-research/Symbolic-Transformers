# Magnitude comparison, precision: close-pair-enriched data

Generated by `python scripts/magnitude_experiment.py --out-dir runs/magnitude_precision --holdout 350-449
--small-gap-frac 0.4 --small-gap-max 10 --epochs 40 --seeds 1 2 3`. Raw numbers: `magnitude_precision.json`.

Same layout as `magnitude_offcenter.md` (values 0-499, 350-449 held out, 500-624 never seen) but 40% of
pairs are drawn with |a − b| ≤ 10, so gaps of 1-10 make up 35% of training instead of about 2%, and the
both-unseen held-out bins at small gaps hold ~1,400 pairs instead of a few dozen. 40 epochs.

## Reading, first batch (learned, grid_linear, grid_ramp_res, grid_ramp_res_fast)

1. **Close-pair training buys seen precision, as intended.** grid_linear goes from 83% to 98.8% at gaps 2-5
   and holds 85.6% at gap 1; the ramp with the fast residual (lr ×10, weight decay 0.1) reaches 90.2% at
   gap 1 and 99.7% at gaps 2-5, the best seen-pair numbers so far. The learned table reaches 58% / 77%.

2. **And it destroys interpolation for every fine-structure encoder.** On both-unseen held-out pairs over
   gap 20, grid_linear falls from 81.7% (uniform data) to 1.4 ± 1.6%, and the fast residual to 2.1 ± 1.4%:
   not chance, systematically inverted. Their comparators learned to read per-cell texture, and cells that
   were never trained have none (grid_linear) or only the block pattern, which reads backwards.

3. **The slow residual keeps interpolation and is the first arm to resolve unseen values finely.**
   grid_ramp_res (lr ×1, weight decay 0.01): 96.3 ± 2.9% over gap 20, 64.0 ± 10.8% at gaps 6-20 and
   54.7 ± 6.3% at gaps 2-5 on both-unseen pairs, where every earlier run sat at chance below gap 20. It keeps
   the far band (95.9% with one far operand). The price is seen precision: 70.6 ± 9.7% at gap 1, 90.3% at
   gaps 2-5, and a higher validation loss (0.125 vs 0.03): it is under-fitting in 40 epochs.

4. **So seen and unseen precision trade off through the residual's strength.** The more the comparator can
   lean on per-cell texture, the sharper it is on trained cells and the worse it reads untrained ones.
